REQUIRED BEHAVIOUR

1. Passive detection while a position is open

The strategy must continue observing and tracking valid new impulse candidates while another strategy position is open.

No pyramiding remains mandatory:

* Never open a second position while the existing position remains open.
* Detection and state tracking must not stop merely because an order is open.
* If the previous position closes before the new impulse reaches its PB1 resumption, the new candidate may remain eligible.
* If the new candidate’s entry signal occurs while the previous position is still open, do not queue a stale entry to execute later. Record that the opportunity was unavailable because the position was busy, then consume it appropriately.

2. Do not lose a seed bar

A qualifying expansion candle processed while a position is open must not disappear permanently.

Track it observationally so the parent leg can continue extending after the position closes.

3. Confirmed-origin recovery without lookahead

If a strong expansion starts before its swing origin becomes confirmed:

* retain a provisional candidate using only already-closed bars;
* when the origin pivot later becomes confirmed, promote the candidate using the closed price history since that pivot;
* do not backdate an entry or pretend the pivot was known earlier;
* only current or future entry decisions may use the newly confirmed information.

4. Oversized expansion bars must not create a blind spot

Preserve the existing meaning of:

A candle exceeding the configured maximum must not independently qualify as a normal seed candle.

5. Parent impulse must dominate contained child impulses

While a substantial parent impulse and its first pullback remain unresolved:

* a smaller same-direction impulse contained within that sequence must not replace the parent;
* the first correction after the larger impulse endpoint remains PB1 of the larger impulse;
* the PB1 high or low must not be relabelled as the origin of a child impulse simply because another directional move follows;
* same-direction seeds within the active parent should extend or strengthen the current parent unless objective evidence establishes a genuinely independent new leg.

Use symmetric bullish and bearish logic.

6. Independent new impulse requirement

A new same-direction impulse may replace the old parent only after the old parent is entered, invalidated, expired or otherwise consumed and a genuinely new confirmed structural origin and directional expansion exist.

Do not allow repeated micro-impulses to move the strategy progressively farther away from the freshest pullback.
