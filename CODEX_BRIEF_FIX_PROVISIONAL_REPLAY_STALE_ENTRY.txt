PROBLEM

The commit named "Fix missed parent impulse and late child entry" added passive detection, provisional candidates and closed-history recovery. The high-level direction is correct, but the promotion replay can still recreate a stale one-generation-late entry.

The defect is in the interaction between TryPromoteCandidate() and RecoverPromotedPullback().

Current behaviour:

1. TryPromoteCandidate() chooses the most extreme high or low over the entire interval from originBar through the current bar.

2. It treats that global final extreme as the parent endpoint before reconstructing PB1.

3. RecoverPromotedPullback() then scans only bars after that endpoint.

4. If PB1 qualified earlier and its resumption also occurred before promotion, RecoverPromotedPullback() records qualificationBar but never evaluates that historical resumption.

5. At the end it always does the equivalent of:

   stage = IMPULSE_ARMED;
   pullbackConfirmed = nbar;

6. The already-passed PB1 is therefore armed at the current bar. A later unrelated same-direction bar can trigger an entry, which is exactly the stale delayed-entry behaviour the preceding fix was meant to prevent.

7. Selecting the global final extreme can also swallow the true first pullback and its resumption into the parent leg. The replay then begins after a later low or high and labels a later correction as PB1.

There is also a narrow oversized-seed defect:

8. An oversized-only candidate is blocked only while nbar <= firstBar.

9. On any later bar, even an opposing candle or doji that adds no same-direction continuation, the original oversized candle's displacement can satisfy the cumulative threshold by itself.

10. That makes the configured maximum-body cap functionally equivalent to a one-bar delay rather than preserving the rule that an oversized candle cannot independently seed an impulse.

These are code-path findings. Do not broaden this job into another general strategy audit.

REQUIRED FIX

1. Chronological promotion replay

When a provisional candidate becomes promotable, reconstruct the state chronologically using only already-closed bars and the same state-transition meanings used by live processing.

Do not select the final global extreme first and then replay only the bars after it.

The chronological reconstruction must distinguish, in order:

* origin available;
* parent expansion and endpoint extension;
* first opposing close beginning PB1;
* a new same-direction extreme resetting an unqualified PB1 and extending the same parent;
* PB1 opposing-close count, retracement, depth, duration and invalidation;
* PB1 qualification;
* the selected aggressive, balanced or conservative resumption after qualification;
* consumption, expiry or invalidation.

The reconstructed endpoint and PB1 must match what the live state machine would have known after each closed bar. Use symmetric bullish and bearish logic.

2. Never re-arm a resumption that already passed

If the recovered PB1 qualified and its selected resumption occurred on a bar before the current promotion decision bar:

* do not enter retrospectively;
* do not set pullbackConfirmed to the current bar merely to keep the setup alive;
* do not queue the old signal for a later bar;
* mark the opportunity consumed/passed with a concise diagnostic such as "PB1 resumption occurred before origin promotion; retrospective entry forbidden";
* prevent a later unrelated continuation candle from triggering that old PB1.

If the resumption occurred while another position was open, the opportunity remains unavailable and consumed. No pyramiding and no delayed execution remain mandatory.

If the resumption occurs on the same currently closed bar that makes promotion possible, handle it explicitly:

* it may be evaluated as a current closed-bar signal only if all required information is available at that bar's close and the normal live entry gates are applied at that decision point; or
* conservatively consume it as unavailable.

Either choice is acceptable, but it must never be converted into an armed setup that waits for a later bar.

If a recovered PB1 has qualified but no resumption has occurred through the current bar, it may be armed for a genuinely future resumption. Preserve the actual historical PB1 qualification bar/time in state and diagnostics; do not claim it qualified on the promotion bar.

3. Do not hide a passed PB1 by moving the endpoint

Once chronological replay has started PB1, a later bar must be interpreted according to the same live rules:

* before PB1 qualification, a genuine new same-direction extreme may reset that unqualified pullback and extend the parent;
* after PB1 qualification, evaluate invalidation, expiry, depth and the chosen resumption;
* do not automatically absorb post-qualification resumption price into a newer endpoint just because it creates the interval's final high or low.

This is essential to preventing the original first pullback from being replaced by a later one.

4. Preserve the maximum-body setting

Keep the existing meaning of:

Impulse maximum body ATR (0 disabled)

An over-cap candle must still be unable to become a normal seed by itself.

An oversized-only provisional candidate may be promoted only when later closed bars provide objective same-direction cumulative evidence. At minimum, require one of the following after the oversized candle:

* a later normal qualifying same-direction seed; or
* later same-direction continuation that advances the impulse endpoint beyond the oversized candle's own extreme as part of a genuine multi-bar leg.

An arbitrary later opposing candle, doji or non-advancing bar must not unlock promotion. The first oversized candle's displacement alone must not satisfy the claimed multi-bar evidence.

Do not add a new user setting or tell the user to change 1.5 to zero.

5. Detection remains separate from execution

Preserve the completed passive-observation work:

* candidates may be observed and parents may be tracked while a strategy position is open;
* no second order may open while the strategy position remains open;
* if the earlier position closes before a not-yet-passed PB1 resumption, the setup may remain eligible;
* a signal that passed while busy or before promotion is never executed later.

6. Preserve parent dominance and independent-leg rules

Do not regress the completed parent/child logic:

* an unresolved parent owns its PB1;
* a contained same-direction child cannot replace it;
* a genuinely independent later same-direction impulse requires the old opportunity to be consumed and a newer confirmed structural origin plus directional expansion;
* bullish and bearish handling must remain symmetric.

For the previously supplied EURUSD semantic sequence:

* the strong bearish move around 18:00-21:00 remains the parent;
* the correction around 00:30-01:00 remains that parent's PB1;
* its first valid resumption is the only entry opportunity for that PB1;
