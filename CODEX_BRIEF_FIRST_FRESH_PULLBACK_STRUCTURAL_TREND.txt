GOAL

Correct Impulse Pullback Continuation mode so it implements the intended strategy:

A substantial multi-candle impulsive move or breakout
→ the first fresh corrective pullback after that complete impulse
→ confirmed price-action resumption
→ entry.

The current FT6 implementation uses a single-candle impulse detector. It can treat a candle or micro-pause inside a larger continuing impulse as a complete impulse/pullback sequence and enter while the parent impulse is still underway.

That behaviour is incorrect.

Do not attempt to improve the historical statistics directly. Correct the strategy semantics first. Do not hardcode any date, time, price, screenshot, instrument or expected result.

AUTHORITATIVE SCREENSHOT-DERIVED EXAMPLE

The supplied EURUSD M15 example around 30 July 2025 represents this sequence:

1. A substantial bearish multi-candle decline begins around the 09:00 area and continues until approximately 13:30.
2. The existing strategy places a sell around 12:00, inside that continuing bearish leg.
3. That sell is premature and must not qualify as a first-pullback entry.
4. The entire decline marked by the user’s blue rectangle is the parent bearish impulse.
5. Only after that impulse stops extending and a corrective bullish phase begins should the strategy track the first pullback.
6. The user’s preferred attempt around the 15:00 area is the type of first-pullback/resumption entry the strategy should consider.
7. That preferred trade may still have been stopped before the later decline. That would be an acceptable trading loss, not a detection failure.
8. The later bearish expansion around 18:30 through the next lower low near 21:00 is a new bearish impulse leg. Its subsequent corrective phase may create another first-pullback opportunity.
9. Individual marubozu candles inside one directional leg must not automatically become unrelated impulses.

REQUIRED IMPULSE STATE MACHINE

Audit the current implementation first and identify the exact state transition that allowed an entry inside the continuing parent impulse.

Implement a symmetric long/short state machine equivalent to:

Idle
→ tracking parent impulse
→ tracking first pullback
→ armed for resumption
→ entered, invalidated or expired
→ consumed until a genuinely new impulse exists.

Required behaviour:

1. A qualifying impulse candle may seed recognition of an impulse, but it must not be treated as the entire impulse by itself.

2. Preserve the exact existing meanings of:

- Impulse minimum body ATR
- Impulse maximum body ATR (0 disabled)
- Impulse minimum range ATR

These remain seed-candle quality controls. Do not silently reinterpret the configured 1.0–1.5 body values as maximum limits on the entire multi-bar leg.

3. Anchor the parent impulse at the appropriate latest confirmed swing origin preceding the qualifying expansion.

4. Extend the parent impulse endpoint whenever price makes another extreme in the impulse direction.

5. Internal pauses and micro-retracements must remain part of the parent impulse unless they genuinely satisfy the configured pullback requirements.

6. Before a pullback becomes qualified, a new impulse-direction extreme must:

- extend the same parent impulse;
- update its endpoint;
- clear/reset the unqualified pullback attempt;
- not generate an entry.

7. A pullback becomes qualified only after satisfying the existing applicable controls, including:

- minimum opposing closes;
- minimum retracement ATR;
- selected depth limits;
- maximum pullback bars;
- invalidation tolerance.

8. After the first pullback qualifies, apply the selected confirmation mode:

Aggressive:
first higher close for longs / first lower close for shorts.

Balanced:
bullish close above the previous high for longs / bearish close below the previous low for shorts.

Conservative:
break of the latest confirmed minor swing in the resumption direction.

Preserve these meanings, but apply them only after a genuine first pullback of the parent impulse has qualified.

9. Permit only the first qualified pullback opportunity belonging to an impulse.

After it enters, expires or is invalidated, do not re-arm from another micro-correction belonging to the same parent leg.

A new opportunity requires a genuinely new impulse leg or newly established structural extension.

10. A horizontal-range breakout is not mandatory in Impulse Pullback Continuation mode.

A valid impulse may:

- break out of a developed range; or
- originate from a confirmed swing and create directional expansion without a clean horizontal range.

Preserve Setup mode 0, the original Range Breakout-Pullback mode, as selectable and behaviourally unchanged.

STRUCTURAL TREND CLASSIFICATION

The existing optional M15/H4 EMA trend filter is not a substitute for structural trend classification. Preserve that filter as its own optional entry filter.

Add a separate real-time price-structure classification using confirmed M15 swing pivots and the existing Minor swing strength unless the current source already has an equivalent reusable pivot implementation.

Use only information available at the current bar.

Classification:

Uptrend:
latest confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding confirmed swing high
AND
latest confirmed swing low is higher than the preceding confirmed swing low.

Downtrend:
latest confirmed swing high is lower than the preceding confirmed swing high
AND
latest confirmed swing low is lower than the preceding confirmed swing low.

Neutral:
mixed structure, equal pivots or insufficient confirmed pivots.

Requirements:

- No lookahead.
- No backdated classification.
- A pivot affects the trend only when it becomes confirmed.
- Do not retrospectively change a trade’s classification.
- Use one symmetric implementation for bullish and bearish structure.
- Log the exact confirmed pivots used for each classification.

In the screenshot, the lower high eventually formed to the right of the preferred entry is future information at that earlier entry time. It must not justify that entry retrospectively. Once that lower high and the following lower low become confirmed, they may establish the downtrend state used by subsequent setups.

DYNAMIC R TARGETS

Two R remains the hard minimum target.

At entry, classify the trade using the structural trend snapshot:

With-trend:

- long while structural trend is Uptrend; or
- short while structural trend is Downtrend.
- Target must be at least 2.0R.

Counter-trend:

- short while structural trend is Uptrend; or
- long while structural trend is Downtrend.
- Target must be at least 3.0R.

Neutral:

- use the normal target, with a hard minimum of 2.0R.

Add or adapt clearly grouped FT6 parameters so the user can configure:

- With-trend / neutral target R, default 2.0, clamped to a minimum of 2.0.
- Counter-trend target R, default 3.0, clamped to a minimum of 3.0.

Do not lower either minimum.

Calculate and freeze the trend classification, risk distance, target multiple and target price at entry. Do not move the target because structural classification later changes.

Preserve existing adaptive/fixed stop behaviour and no-pyramiding behaviour.
