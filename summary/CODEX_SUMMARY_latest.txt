Trading Journal focused cleanup revision summary

Implemented:
- Removed the five-win/five-trade target recommendation threshold from the active recommendation path. A scope is now target-eligible with at least one eligible win and at least one eligible loss.
- Added exact target empty reasons: "No eligible winning trades" and "No eligible losing trades".
- Removed neutral target/stop outputs from generated recommendations. Exact stop ties prefer a small decrease; exact target ties prefer a small increase and record tie-preference diagnostics.
- Fixed target baselines so planned target R is calculated only from the same eligible winning rows used for target recommendation comparisons.
- Kept target recommendation losses out of the captured-R histogram while requiring at least one eligible loss.
- Kept the adaptive target histogram rules: Freedman-Diaconis width snapped to 0.25R/0.5R/1.0R, floor bucketing, lower modal bucket on ties, and raw median of the modal bucket.
- Tightened captured-R provenance so target captured R prefers net profit divided by verified original monetary risk, rejects cost/partial-exit price fallbacks, and only uses price-derived R when it is net-equivalent.
- Expanded shared test-trade exclusion to top-level, alternate marker fields, metrics, raw refs, and source_data.
- Removed `_Target R Metadata` generation and hidden metadata rehydration; legacy helper sheets are removed on refresh.
- Preserved prior repairs for Stats 1 freeze/drawdown/leaders, Stats 2 Net P/L conditional formatting, one-line P&L Calendar, Trade Log row heights, and no Trade Log recommendation columns.
