2. Blocking errors versus supplementary warnings
- /v3/accounts discovery failure is now retained as a non-blocking warning instead of a source error.
- When the configured account's current openTrades and pendingOrders requests succeed, its rows remain current, the payload is not stale, error_count remains zero, and warning_count identifies the discovery warning.
- The warning explicitly says that additional owned-account discovery failed. Without a last-known-good list, it says only the configured account's current data was confirmed.
- Required account-data failures remain blocking. A failed openTrades or pendingOrders request produces a red source error, marks the payload stale, identifies the account and exact endpoint, and preserves rows from the other successful endpoint.
- Failure of a discovered secondary account is also blocking while successful configured-account, other OANDA-account, and Bybit rows remain present.
- Cached partial/error payloads no longer become incorrectly non-stale during the Open Orders response-cache lifetime.
- The payload now exposes errors, warnings, error_count, warning_count, and matching source_counts fields. stale continues to derive only from blocking errors.

3. OANDA account-discovery cache and diagnostics
- The fresh owned-account cache default is now one hour instead of 20 seconds and is configurable with OANDA_ACCOUNTS_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS.
- A last-known-good owned-account list is retained for transient failures for one day by default, configurable with OANDA_ACCOUNTS_STALE_TTL_SECONDS and bounded to seven days.
- Stale membership is used only to choose account IDs. Current openTrades and pendingOrders are always fetched for each configured/cached account; positions and orders themselves are never served from the discovery cache.
- Non-transient discovery failures such as HTTP 401 do not use stale membership.
- Concurrent refreshes for the same base/token identity share one in-flight request. Cache keys hash the credentials rather than retaining a token suffix.
- Discovery remains bounded at three attempts with a six-second per-request timeout and short existing backoffs.
- Timeout, request/transport, DNS, HTTP status, rate-limit, invalid-response, attempt count, retry exhaustion, timeout, and elapsed-attempt diagnostics are explicit and sanitized. Tokens and credential-bearing URLs are not returned.
- A missing OandaUpstreamError class was restored so required account endpoint timeout/transport failures retain useful sanitized retry-exhaustion diagnostics instead of degrading into a NameError.
