Trading Journal regression repair summary

Root causes found:
- Target recommendations rejected legacy FX winners when monetary opening loss conversion metadata was absent, even when immutable original entry, stop, target, and actual exit prices were present. This made FX unavailable and left Stats 1/Symbols inconsistent.
- Stop-loss recommendations used older distance/average plumbing and direction-only text, with broad neutral behavior. They were not consistently based on original stop-loss percentage distance, and service/Symbols/Stats 1 had divergent implementations.
- Stats 1 freeze panes were dropped during workbook repair; historical audit recovered the intended `B88` freeze pane.
- Stats 1 Drawdown and Most Wins/Most Losses could be blank when upstream stats omitted those fields; the generator lacked row-derived fallbacks.
- Stats 1 Pattern “Most Profitable” and “Least Profitable” rows were included in generated profit/loss semantic formatting ranges, causing red/green styling on text category cells.
- Stats 2 Net P/L Percentage values were written without a durable dynamic profit/loss conditional-formatting range.
- Trade Log row heights were normalized to a constant instead of the live row-5 data height after later copy/style operations, allowing rows such as 28 to stay oversized.
- P&L Calendar build/update paths preserved the broken two-row layout: duplicated month labels, `{year} Trades` rows, and separate trade-count cells.

Calculation rules implemented:
- Target: realised winning trades only; exclude tests, losses, break-even/zero-R, non-trade/revaluation/cashflow rows, invalid values, missing/untrusted original stops or targets, and moved-plan rows without immutable original levels. Use monetary original risk when available; otherwise use captured price-R from original entry/stop and actual exit. Minimum eligible wins is 5. Histogram uses Freedman-Diaconis bin width snapped to 0.25R/0.5R/1.0R, floor bucketing, lower bucket on ties, and raw-value median of the modal bucket. Direction uses half-bin tolerance versus median original planned target R.
- Stop: genuine realised winners and losers only; use original stop-loss percentage distance, not pips and not moved/final levels. Requires at least one eligible win and one eligible loss. Recommendation percentage is the winning mean, with increase/decrease/equal direction from unrounded means and displayed percentage-point gap.
