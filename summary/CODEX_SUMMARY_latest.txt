Drawdown audit conclusion:
- The old report/workbook fallback based on cumulative per-trade percentages was wrong for account drawdown because it could treat deposits, withdrawals, or revaluations as trading drawdowns.
- The corrected definition uses per-account balance/equity points, preferring analysis_balance_after_trade and falling back to balance_after_trade, segmented by account and cashflow/monthly AUD revaluation anchors.
- A drawdown observation is prior peak balance to later trough balance within the same account/anchor segment. Start is the peak timestamp; End is the later trough timestamp.
- Independent recalculation from the authorized repo workbook matched the audit clue:
  Overall min 0.008612%, avg 10.132670%, max 45.231131%.
  Forex min 0.013839%, avg 5.042601%, max 23.403578%.
  Crypto min 0.008612%, avg 22.618807%, max 45.231131%.
- Final workbook checks confirmed populated drawdown values use distinct peak/trough timestamps where the data supports it.
