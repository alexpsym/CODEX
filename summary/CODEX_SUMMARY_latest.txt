Trading Journal Resync row-dimension preservation fix

- Root cause: the preservation-mode Trade Log rebuild repopulates rows in generated order. When a surviving row moves, presentation restoration followed the stable Row ID for cell formatting but restored only its height; its other RowDimension fields remained at the old Excel coordinate.
- The restore now snapshots and relocates the complete RowDimension state by stable Row ID, including height, hidden/outline/collapsed flags, row style state, thickTop, and thickBot. Identity-owned source dimensions are removed before their saved state is applied to the matching surviving row, preventing metadata from leaking to the row now occupying the old coordinate.
- The strict preservation contract and transactional candidate/rollback behavior are unchanged. No real workbook or calculation-context JSON was altered, and no general performance change was made because the inspected defect did not establish a separate repeated-work cause for the full rebuild duration.
- Targeted verification passed once: `tests/test_master_journal_workbook.py::test_preservation_mode_moves_trade_presentation_with_row_id_after_monthly_dedupe` (`1 passed`). The test moves a surviving row and verifies its full custom row-dimension signature, including `height=15.0`, `style_id=0`, and `thickBot=True`, survives the preservation validation.
- Changed only `tools/master_journal_workbook.py`, `tests/test_master_journal_workbook.py`, and this summary.
