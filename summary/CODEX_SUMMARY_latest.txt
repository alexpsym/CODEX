A rejected attempt requires a newly generated .set/token.
- Market entry is anchored to the live Ask for buys or Bid for sells. Volume, SL, TP, commission-aware risk, and Net-RR are recalculated from the live execution anchor. OrdersEnabled, one-trade enforcement, trade/symbol readiness, volume min/step/max, stop/freeze levels, deviation, magic number, and risk limits are enforced.

It now prevents duplicate attempts across callbacks, observes the returned pending-order ticket, retries only transient readiness/transport failures with bounded exponential backoff (six attempts), stops on structural invalidity, and reports accepted-but-not-observed mismatches.
