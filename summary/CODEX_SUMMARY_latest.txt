Implementation:
- Added shared `MIN_ELIGIBLE_TARGET_R_WINS = 5`.
- Added an explicit `scope` parameter to `_target_r_recommendation`; Overall callers now pass `scope="overall"`.
- Overall coverage now uses:
  - `fx_sufficient = eligible_fx_wins >= MIN_ELIGIBLE_TARGET_R_WINS`
  - `crypto_sufficient = eligible_crypto_wins >= MIN_ELIGIBLE_TARGET_R_WINS`
- Overall recommendations now follow the required matrix:
  - both sufficient: normal Overall direction/recommended target format;
  - Crypto sufficient and FX insufficient: `Crypto-only data — Recommended: <value>R — FX: insufficient eligible wins`;
  - FX sufficient and Crypto insufficient: `FX-only data — Recommended: <value>R — Crypto: insufficient eligible wins`;
  - neither sufficient: `Insufficient eligible wins — recommended target unavailable`.
- When Overall is Crypto-only or FX-only, the displayed recommended R is calculated from the sufficient asset class only, so one to four insufficient eligible wins from the other class cannot influence the recommendation.
- Symbol recommendations, FX-only STATS1, and Crypto-only STATS1 remain on the standard non-Overall target-R format.
- Workbook-derived STATS1 calculations now pass Overall scope when recomputing the Overall market bucket from workbook rows.
