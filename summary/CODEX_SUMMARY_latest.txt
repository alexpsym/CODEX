Brief: Add configurable extension-relative pullback-depth modes to the TradingView range breakout-pullback strategy.
Recorded: 2026-08-27 (Australia/Brisbane)

- Added pinescripts/range_breakout_pullback_strategy.pine as a Pine Script v6 overlay strategy with independent long/short controls, pyramiding disabled, confirmation-close market entries, and Strategy Tester output.
- The transparent range detector clusters confirmed pivot highs and lows around running ATR-tolerant centres, requires at least two reactions on both boundaries by default, enforces minimum ATR height and configurable duration/staleness, and freezes both boundaries at breakout.
- Each trade follows confirmed range, buffered directional breakout, later-bar extension, corrective pullback, and a later completed-bar resumption. Pullbacks still need opposing closes and ATR retracement, but former-boundary proximity no longer gates eligibility.
- Added Any valid depth, Shallow, Deep and Custom range modes. Bullish depth is (frozen extension high - pullback low) / (frozen extension high - broken resistance); bearish depth is its exact inverse. The extension extreme freezes on the first confirmed opposing close, bounded modes arm at their minimum and disqualify above their maximum, and optional entry labels show the confirmed depth.
- Resumption modes are Aggressive, Balanced (default), and Conservative confirmed minor-swing breaks. Entries are never backdated to the pullback extreme.
- Adaptive ATR stops default to ATR(14) with current/prior-only ATR%-percentile regimes and 1.0/1.5/2.0 multipliers; fixed-multiple mode is also available. ATR and multiplier are snapshotted at entry, and tick-rounded stop and default 2R target prices are frozen for the trade.
- Transferred the recurring FX Weekend schedule from fxweekend-clone/liquidate.py and fxweekend-clone/settings.json: flat/no-entry from Friday 15:00 through Sunday 16:59 America/New_York, reopening Sunday 17:00. This exactly represents Saturday 05:00 Brisbane while New York is on DST and Saturday 06:00 Brisbane during New York standard time. The strategy cancels unfilled strategy orders, closes open positions, and optionally shades the blocked window.
