Target recommendation correction:
- Removed the circular FX monetary-risk reconstruction that derived conversion from realised P/L and exit movement.
- FX target-R now requires independently established original monetary risk:
  - known-currency stored original/planned monetary risk,
  - verified original calculator/quote estimated_total_loss with known matching currency,
  - OANDA independent loss conversion factors such as homeConversionFactors.loss, plHomeConversionFactors.loss, or exported CONVERSION RATE.
- FX trades without independent original monetary risk are excluded with missing_independent_original_monetary_risk.
- FX monetary risk is now absolute(original entry - original stop) * original units * independent loss conversion factor.
- Realised commissions, financing, swap, guaranteed-stop fees and other costs remain in the net P/L numerator only.
- Unknown risk currency is no longer treated as compatible.
- Broad/unsafe stored-risk fallbacks such as max_loss, generic risk_amount, generic risk_cash, generic risk_money, and generic estimated_loss are no longer accepted.
- Crypto target-R still uses net P/L divided by independently reconstructed original monetary risk from original price risk, quantity, multiplier and quote currency.

OANDA data preservation:
- OANDA transaction-history CSV import now preserves exported CONVERSION RATE from open and close rows in metrics/raw_refs.
- OANDA API/fill row construction now preserves homeConversionFactors and plHomeConversionFactors in metrics/raw_refs.
- Existing OANDA financing, commission, GSL fee/premium and swap/net-profit handling remains intact.
